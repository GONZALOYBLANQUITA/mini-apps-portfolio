Medical affairs workspace designed around the operating model described by Veeva Vault CRM for Medical: a single platform connecting teams through a unified customer database, stakeholder profiling, content sharing and management, territory and account planning, and field engagement with insights capture and analysis. The official product page also states browser and desktop access with full offline support on iPad, iPhone, and Android.
Engagement model
Plan, execute, and track scientific interactions across channels. Share relevant medical information, capture field insights in real time, and align with internal counterparts for cohesive engagement.

Improve scientific exchange, streamline content dissemination, gather actionable medical insights, and collaborate compliantly.
